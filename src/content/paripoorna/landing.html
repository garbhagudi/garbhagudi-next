Questions couples often ask us

Still unsure about something? Call us on 9108 9108 32.

Does everyone experiencing difficulty conceiving need IVF?
No. Fertility concerns can have different causes and appropriate approaches. An individual clinical evaluation helps determine whether lifestyle changes, medication, IUI, IVF, another treatment, or further observation may be appropriate.
What is the procedure to register for Paripoorna 2026?
Please visit our branches for a fertility specialist consultation or contact our GarbhaGudi Care support team on 9108 9108 32. We'll guide you through the steps and help you complete the process smoothly.
Who is eligible for Paripoorna 2026?
It is designed for couples seeking IVF treatment who need financial support. Paripoorna 2026 is relevant only when a qualified fertility specialist advises IVF and the couple meets the applicable conditions. A consultation does not automatically lead to IVF.
Is everyone who registers entitled to Paripoorna 2026?
A couple becomes eligible for Paripoorna 2026 after completing registration, consultation with a fertility specialist, receiving a diagnosis, and being prescribed a treatment plan suited to their needs.
Are medicines included in Paripoorna 2026?
Yes. Ovarian stimulation injections are included in Paripoorna 2026. The full list of inclusions is shown in the "What's included" section, and our financial advisor will explain it before you begin.
If eligible, will I receive a treatment plan?
Yes, you will receive the treatment plan from the fertility specialist.
Can I continue my treatment without opting for the Paripoorna 2026 program?
Yes. This also helps other couples in genuine need to avail Paripoorna 2026.
I have started treatment at another facility. Can I still apply?
Yes, you can apply. However, we recommend that you do not discontinue your ongoing treatment without discussing it with your doctor, as it could affect your treatment.
Can I register online without visiting the centre?
No. Please visit the nearest GarbhaGudi IVF Centre for a fertility specialist consultation.
Until when can I apply for Paripoorna 2026?
For current program availability, please contact our GarbhaGudi Care support team on 9108 9108 32 or visit your nearest GarbhaGudi IVF Centre.
